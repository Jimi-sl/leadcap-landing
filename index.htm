Frequently Asked Questions

Which ad platforms does LeadCap support? +

LeadCap connects ad accounts across Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat, and X, and brings their performance data together into one unified dashboard instead of you logging into each platform's native ads manager separately.

Is there a free trial, and do I need a card to sign up? +

Yes - every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. A card is required at signup, but you won't be charged until day 30, so you have the full trial period to decide before any payment is taken.

What's the difference between the Brand and Agency plans? +

The Brand Plan (£35/month after trial) is built for a single brand, with up to 3 connected social accounts and a basic analytics dashboard. The Agency Plan (£110/month after trial) supports unlimited connected accounts and multi-client management, with white-label reports, priority support, and API access - built for agencies managing several clients at once.

Does LeadCap use AI? +

Yes - Captain Guru, LeadCap's built-in AI assistant, summarizes your ad performance and answers questions about your spend, reach, and engagement across every connected platform, right from the dashboard.
